### Q7. Great Flowering and the Festival

Shoot flowering: shoots are 35% of food under 60 colonists, 20% above; dead plants are cleared by gathering their seed (3 each). *_replant_now: replanting allowed at once (growth + clearing); *_season: that bamboo cannot be replanted for the rest of the temperate season (~15 days), which is what 'a food season to bridge' needs. Poles at 0.35 a colonist a day. Seed cakes keep the +3 dish need for everyone for cake_days.

Frequency: with the first at cycle 6 and then every 5-7 cycles (alternating), a 30-cycle game sees about 4-5 Flowerings, 2-3 of each bamboo; each costs one temperate season of adjustment.

Small Festival in cycle 2: science for Steamer, Pickle Yard, Paddy House (three cooked dishes: steamed shoots, pickles, steamed rice), Paper Mill, Lantern Workshop, Festival Square = 1060; two inventors at wellbeing 10 (x1.4) make 45 a day, so about day 28, i.e. the start of cycle 2 (cycle 1 = 13-17 temperate + 2-3 drought). 40 lanterns = 40 paper + 20 poles = 40 poles.
