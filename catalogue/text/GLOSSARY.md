# Glossary: one name per thing, one word per job

For writers and translators of *Pandaborn - Factions*. It covers every text a player reads: `catalogue/text/*.json`
(names, descriptions, tutorial), the module files `src/code/Pandaborn.*/mod/Localizations/enUS_*.csv` (panels,
statuses, notifications) and the Words table (`src/code/Pandaborn.Words/words.review.enUS.txt`). Voice and form are
in [STYLE.md](STYLE.md). Written by an AI in the text pass of 2026-11-25; not yet reviewed by the owner.

A translator should give each term below one translation and keep it everywhere. Two English words for one thing
were the most common fault this pass removed.

## The peoples

| Term | Meaning | Where | Never |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inkpaw, Inkpaws | The giant pandas, and the faction's name. They work from dawn to the afternoon bell | Faction screen, the bell's texts, the one rule | "pandas" when only the giant pandas are meant |
| Ringtail, Ringtails | The red pandas. They arrive with the first Moon Loft and work from the bell to dawn | Moon Loft, night posts, the one rule | red pandas, helpers, servants, little ones, "for the Inkpaws" |
| panda, pandas | Anyone who lives in the village, of either people. The word that stands where vanilla says "beaver" | Descriptions, statuses, the Words table | villager, colonist, beaver, citizen, settler |
| cub, cubs | Young Inkpaws | Dens, flavor | kit, child (for an Inkpaw) |
| kit, kits | Young Ringtails, raised in Moon Lofts | Moon Loft panel | cub, pup |
| young panda; the young | Cubs and kits together | Shared labels, Kindergarten | children |
| the village | The settlement | Everywhere | colony, settlement, town |
| both peoples | Inkpaws and Ringtails together | Wonder text | both species, both races |
| Wooden Ox, Wooden Oxen | The faction's bots | Ox Workshop, bot names | robot, bamboobot |

## The day

| Term | Meaning | Where | Never |
|---|---|---|---|
| the afternoon bell; then "the bell" | The end of the Inkpaws' working day, set on the game's working-hours control. Say "afternoon bell" the first time in a text, "the bell" after | Tutorial, panels, the one rule | gong, shift bell, slider |
| working day | The Inkpaws' hours, from dawn to the bell | Bell texts | shift (for Inkpaws) |
| rested; Rested | The bonus to working and walking speed on a shorter working day. Capital R only as the panel label | Working-hours panel, tooltip | refreshed, energized, weary (there is no penalty) |
| the one rule | "Inkpaws work from dawn to the bell. Ringtails work from the bell to dawn." Quote it whole | Arrival message, Moon Loft tutorial stage | any paraphrase |
| night post | A Ringtail workplace: Night Builders' Post, Night Courier Post, Night Gatherers' Post, Star Tower | Arrival message, panels | night shift building, moon building |
| vacancy | A free place for a worker at a night post (the vanilla word) | Moon Loft panel | open night job |
| Night Watch | The toolbar group that holds the Ringtail buildings | Toolbar | night crew |
| off duty | Not at work, at whatever hour | Teahouse, Opera Stage | "in the evening" (Ringtails are off duty in the morning) |

Never promise more work from a shorter day: within the bonus's range the day's output is the same, and the choice
is about free time. Never type an hour or a percentage into a text: a number appears only as a parameter the code prints from the data.

## Words that never appear in player text

beaver (in any text written for this mod), villager, colonist, module, spec, template, stand-in, prototype, feature
(say "part of the mod"), oriental, and any religious word. No Chinese characters: they appear only in the checking
column of `names.json`.

## Kept from vanilla, as vanilla writes them

badwater, drought, badtide, well-being, district, levee, dam, floodgate, Science Points, unemployed, vacancy,
contaminated. A player should never relearn a levee, and these are already translated in every language.
