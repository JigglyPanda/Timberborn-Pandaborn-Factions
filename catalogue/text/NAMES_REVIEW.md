# Colonist names: the list for a native reader

The names in `names.json` were written by an AI and have not been read by a native speaker of Mandarin. In the game a
colonist shows the pinyin and the English gloss only, for example "Mo Mo (Ink)"; the characters are here so that a reader
can check them. This table holds every entry the writer was not sure of (71 of 253): the ones marked medium or low, the
ones whose toneless spelling is shared with another word, and the Ringtail names taken from dishes and condiments.
The other entries are marked high and are worth one quick read as well.

For each line, please answer: is it a real name or pet name with this meaning, does it sound natural, and does it call
anything unwelcome to mind? Write your answer in the last column. An entry the reader rejects is deleted from
`names.json`; nothing else depends on it, except the four cast names in `STYLE.md` (Nuo Nuo, Song Song, Dou Dou, Hua Jiao).
