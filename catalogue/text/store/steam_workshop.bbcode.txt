[h1]Pandaborn - Factions: the Inkpaws[/h1]

Giant pandas who would rather lead a river than dam it.

The Inkpaws run open bamboo flumes down to rice terraces and grow tea on the dry ground beavers leave empty. They work a short day and work it well. When the afternoon bell rings they put down their tools, and the Ringtails, the red pandas of the Night Watch, take over the village until dawn.

A whole new faction, unlocked from the start. Needs no other mods. Folktails and Iron Teeth are not touched.

[b]Early access.[/b] Everything from the first den to the wonder is in. Balance is a first pass, and the late game has only been tested by scripts. What is still being tested is listed below, plainly.

[h2]How they play[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]The first hour is the game you know.[/b] Water pump, gatherer, lumberjack, farmhouse. The first crop is shoot bamboo, eaten raw. Levees, dams and floodgates keep their names.
[*][b]Poles, not planks.[/b] Timber bamboo is a fast tree, and cut bamboo is already a pole. A Pole Mill splits logs for the first days and for long droughts.
[*][b]The afternoon bell.[/b] It is the working-hours control you already have. The shorter the day, the faster rested pandas work and walk, and from twelve hours to sixteen the work done comes out the same. So you choose: more hours, or an afternoon of hammocks and tea.
[*][b]Every kind of land has a use.[/b] Rice in flooded fields. Bamboo, cabbage and persimmons on green ground. Tea and Sichuan pepper on brown ground, where a drought cannot reach them.
[*][b]Lead the water.[/b] A flume carries water one way, level or downhill, and keeps the ground beside it moist. A Scoop Wheel lifts water out of a current with no workers. A Paddy Spout holds a rice field at a shallow depth. The Flume Gate shuts itself when badwater comes.
[*][b]Two peoples, one village.[/b] Build a Moon Loft, a snug home on stilts with a lantern in its window, and a Ringtail family moves in. Ringtails work from the bell to dawn: they build, carry and gather through the night, and run with a full basket. A village that never builds a Moon Loft is still complete.
[*][b]The Lantern Festival.[/b] Stock up on paper lanterns and cooked dishes, call the festival, and the whole village takes a day and a night off to dance in the square.
[*][b]The Great Flowering.[/b] Once in many cycles one of the two bamboos flowers, bears seed and dies back. You are told a full cycle ahead. It can be made rare or switched off.
[/list]

[h2]What is in it[/h2]
[list]
[*]About 170 buildings: the Folktails' staples in the Inkpaws' own look, and nearly sixty of the faction's own on new models. Dark timber, pale plaster, grey clay tile, bamboo, and lattice windows that glow at night.
[*]Panda bodies for adults and cubs, and a red panda body for the Ringtails, each with several coats.
[*]Seven new plants and a Sichuan kitchen: steamed shoots, pickles, steamed rice, hot pot, sweet rice balls.
[*]Afternoon places: Hammock, Nap Platform, Teahouse, Slide and Swing, Kindergarten, Ink Studio, Hot Spring Bath.
[*]Pandas with real names and their meaning: Le Le (Joy), Xing He (River of stars).
[*]The interface says Inkpaw, cub and panda where the game says beaver and kit. Only in Inkpaws games.
[*]Wooden Oxen for bots, kite windmills and bamboo water wheels for power, a cliff carving of a seated panda, and a Sky Lantern for a wonder.
[*]Its own sounds: the bell, the night watchman's clapper, water in a flume.
[/list]

[h2]Still being tested[/h2]
[list]
[*]The Trip-Hammer Mill and the Treadwheel Lift have not yet been run with a worker in a game.
[*]The Ox Workshop, the monuments and the Sky Lantern are built and placed in tests. No test has reached them by playing.
[*]The festival's rising lanterns use the game's firework trails until their own art is finished.
[*]The Star Tower, where Ringtails make science at night, is new and has not been run in a game yet. The Opera Stage is a plain attraction on a stand-in model.

[h2]Saves[/h2]
[list]
[*]Adds one faction. Folktails and Iron Teeth keep their buildings, coats and text. No file of the game is replaced. Safe to add to any game.
[*][b]Removing the mod breaks Inkpaws saves.[/b] Other saves are not affected. After a Timberborn update, wait for this mod's update before loading an Inkpaws save.
[*]If a game update breaks one part of the mod, that part switches off, the game says so, and the save still loads.
[*]English text. A Simplified Chinese translation is included; it was made by AI and has not been checked by a native reader.
[/list]

[h2]Works with, and known issues[/h2]
[list]
[*]Uses no Harmony and needs no other mod. Mod Settings is not needed: the Great Flowering is set per save, in the game.
[*][b]Use one copy only.[/b] A Workshop copy plus a manual copy makes TimberUi (needed by TImprove and Choo!Choo!) stop the game at start.
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3838695858]Pandaborn - Panda Reskin[/url]: works. Update it too, so the Inkpaws keep their own portraits and the Ringtails get their own fur.
[*]Building packs (Staircase, Ladder, More Platforms, Path Extention, Water Extention, Extended Floodgates, Flywheels, Ferris Wheel, Food Stall and others) keep working for Folktails and Iron Teeth. Their buildings are not offered to the Inkpaws, because those mods list their buildings per faction. Their authors can add the Inkpaws with one small file: the collection is Buildings.Inkpaws.
[*]Choo!Choo! is available to the Inkpaws.
[*]Mods that change a vanilla building do not change the Inkpaws' own version of it.
[*]Automation and Simple Floodgate Triggers work on Inkpaws floodgates and pumps. They cannot control the Flume Gate.
[*]Performance mods (T3MP, Performance Plus) and other faction mods: not tested yet. If something looks wrong in an Inkpaws game, try without them first and tell us.
[*]If one part of the mod clashes with another mod, you can switch that part off: put a text file named PandabornFactions.txt in Documents\Timberborn with a line such as "off: Flowering". The names are in the guide on GitHub. This switch is new and has not been run in a game yet.
[*]In a bug report, include the line of the game's log that starts "[Pandaborn] built for game version".
[/list]
