## Summary

The cozy panda faction. Run bamboo aqueducts down to rice terraces, ring the afternoon bell, and let the red pandas of the Night Watch take over the village until dawn. No other mods needed.

## Description

**Pandaborn - Factions** adds the Inkpaws, a new playable faction of giant pandas, unlocked from the start.

**They lead the water.** Open bamboo flumes carry water one way, level or downhill, to groves and rice terraces.
Scoop wheels lift it with no workers. Levees, dams and floodgates keep their usual names.

**They ring the afternoon bell.** The shorter the working day, the faster rested pandas work and walk. Afternoons
are for hammocks and tea. Long days are allowed; they only leave no time for tea and naps.

**Two peoples, one village.** Build a Moon Loft and a family of Ringtails moves in. The Ringtails are the
village's Night Watch, its stargazers and its artists, and they work from the bell to dawn.

Also: tea and Sichuan pepper on dry ground, a Sichuan kitchen from steamed shoots to hot pot, the Lantern
Festival, the Great Flowering, Wooden Oxen and a Sky Lantern.

**What it changes, and what it does not**

* Adds one faction. Folktails and Iron Teeth play exactly as before, and no file of the game is replaced.
* Needs no other mods. Works with or without *Pandaborn - Panda Reskin*.
* Safe to add at any time. **Removing the mod breaks Inkpaws saves**; other saves are not affected.
* Windows and Mac. English text.
