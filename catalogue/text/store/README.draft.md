# Pandaborn - Factions: the Inkpaws

A new playable faction for [Timberborn](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1062090/Timberborn/). The Inkpaws are
giant pandas who run bamboo aqueducts down to rice terraces. When the afternoon bell rings, they put down their
tools, and the red pandas take over the village for the night.

Unlocked from the start. Needs no other mods.

## What makes them different

**They lead the water.** A flume is an open bamboo aqueduct, one tile wide. Water runs one way along it, level
or downhill, and it never leaks. A Scoop Wheel lifts water out of the river with no workers, and a Paddy Spout
holds a rice field at a shallow depth. Levees, dams and floodgates keep their usual names and behavior.

**They ring the afternoon bell.** The bell is the game's own working-hours control. The shorter the day, the
faster rested pandas work and walk: at twelve hours they get as much done as beavers do in sixteen. Afternoons
are for hammocks, tea, the hot spring and watching the cubs. Long days are allowed; they only leave no time for
tea and naps.

**Two peoples, one village.** Build a Moon Loft and a family of Ringtails moves in. The Ringtails are the
village's Night Watch, its stargazers and its artists. They work from the bell to dawn: night builders, night
couriers, foragers, the Star Tower and the Opera Stage. A village with no Ringtails is still complete.

## Also in the valley

* **Every kind of land has a use.** Rice in flooded fields, bamboo and cabbage on moist ground, tea and Sichuan
  pepper on dry ground only.
* **Bamboo, twice.** Shoot bamboo is the first crop. Timber bamboo is a fast tree cut for poles, and poles need
  no mill.
* **A Sichuan kitchen.** Steamed shoots, pickles, steamed rice, tea, sweet rice balls and hot pot.
* **The Lantern Festival.** Stock the square, call the festival, and everyone takes a day and a night off.
* **The Great Flowering.** Once in many cycles, one kind of bamboo flowers and dies. It is announced a full
  cycle ahead, it leaves behind the best food in the valley, and it can be made rare or switched off.
* **Late game.** Wooden Oxen, kite windmills, a great cliff carving of a seated panda, and a Sky Lantern that
  carries seeds over the wasteland.
* **Real names.** Colonists are called things like Mo Mo (Ink) and Xing He (River of stars).

## Install

1. Subscribe on the Steam Workshop or mod.io, or download the release and unzip it into
   `Documents\Timberborn\Mods\`. You should end up with `Mods\PandabornFactions\manifest.json`.
2. Enable **Pandaborn - Factions** in the game's mod manager.
3. Start a new game and choose the Inkpaws.

## What it changes, and what it does not

* Adds one faction, the Inkpaws, with its own buildings, goods, crops and needs.
* Folktails and Iron Teeth play exactly as before. No file of the game is replaced.
* Needs no other mods. It works with or without
  [Pandaborn - Panda Reskin](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3838695858).
* Safe to add at any time.
* **Removing the mod breaks Inkpaws saves.** A game played as the Inkpaws needs the mod to load. Games played as
  other factions are not affected.
* Windows and Mac. Game version 1.1 or later.
* The text is in English, written plainly so that machine translation reads well.
